Before Your Card Kicks In
Waiting periods you should know about
Accidental Injury — Covered Immediately
No waiting period. Coverage starts from day one, 24 hours a day.
All Other Illnesses — 30 Days
General illnesses are covered 30 days after your certificate starts.
Specified Illnesses — 120 Days
Conditions such as hypertension and diabetes need 120 days before they're covered.
Critical Illness Benefit — 60 Days
Heart attack, coronary conditions and cancer under the Critical Illness benefit need 60 days.
